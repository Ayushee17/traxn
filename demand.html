Demand generation and growth

Turn a story that works into a pipeline that grows.

Demand generation is where strategy meets the calendar. We design programmes around how your buyers actually research and decide, run the first experiments with you, and measure what moved pipeline rather than what made noise.

Demand strategy→Campaigns→Conversion→Measurement

1 · The problem

Lots of activity. Not much you can trace to revenue.

The team posts, sends, publishes and runs ads. Traffic goes up. Form fills come in. But the pipeline review shows the same handful of deals, most of them from referrals and the founder's network.

Two things are usually wrong. The programmes were chosen because they're what B2B companies do, not because they match how your buyers research. And nothing connects the activity to the pipeline, so there's no way to cut what isn't working.

Volume doesn't fix either. Doubling output on the wrong channel doubles the cost of being wrong.

2 · What it achieves

Fewer programmes, chosen on evidence, measured properly.

A demand strategy

Two or three programmes chosen from how your buyers research and who influences them, with a clear reason for each.

Campaigns that tell one story

Content, outbound and founder-led programmes that all prove the same position, so every touch reinforces the last.

Conversion experiments

Landing-page and funnel tests with written hypotheses, so a result teaches you something either way.

A measurement model

A simple model that connects activity to qualified pipeline and makes the monthly review end in a decision.

3 · Our process

A three-week strategy phase, then a monthly programme.

Understand · Week 1

Map the buyer's journey

Where do your best customers first hear of you? What do they read, ask and compare before a first call? We find out from closed-won data, calls and interviews.

Define · Week 2

Choose the programmes

Two or three programmes that fit the journey, each with a hypothesis, a budget and the signal that would prove it right or wrong.

Build · Week 3

Plan the first quarter

Campaign briefs, landing-page and outbound drafts, the measurement model and a 90-day calendar with owners.

Learn · Monthly

Run, review, decide

Optional monthly programme (three-month minimum): we run experiments with your team, review pipeline impact and decide what to scale, fix or stop.

7 · Questions

Asked before most demand projects.

Do you run the campaigns yourselves?

In the monthly programme, we run the strategy, briefs, experiments and review, and write the first versions of content and outbound. Larger production volumes come from your team or contractors we help you brief.

Which channels do you recommend?

The ones your buyers use. For most B2B SaaS companies that's some mix of founder-led content, targeted outbound, partnerships and search, but we decide from the buyer journey, not from a default list.

How soon will we see results?

Leading indicators (reply rates, qualified first calls) move within four to eight weeks. Closed revenue follows your sales cycle. We set expectations against your cycle length before we start.

What does it cost?

The strategy phase is fixed-price. The monthly programme is a fixed retainer with a three-month minimum and a scope agreed each quarter. You get a one-page proposal after the first call.
