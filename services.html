Home/What we do

Four services, one way of thinking.

Everything Traxn does starts with understanding your customers and ends with decisions your team can act on. Start where the problem is loudest; the services connect when you need them to.

Start with positioning if…

people don't understand what you do, you lose deals to weaker products, or your team pitches it three different ways.

Start with go-to-market if…

you're launching something new, entering a new market, or sales and marketing are working from different plans.

Start with demand generation if…

the story is right but the pipeline isn't growing, or you can't tell which activity is producing revenue.

Start with AI search if…

buyers research with ChatGPT and Perplexity and those tools don't mention you, or mention you wrongly.

Service 1

Brand and product positioning

Decide who you serve, what you replace and why you win. Then say it in words buyers already use.

Customer understanding→Differentiation→Value proposition→Messaging

  • Customer and lost-deal research to find who chooses you and why
  • Competitive alternatives mapped from the buyer's point of view
  • A ranked set of positioning options, with the risk of each
  • A one-page messaging guide and rewritten homepage copy

Positioning in detail

Typical engagement

Two to three weeks. Two working sessions with your team, six to eight hours of your time in total.

Best for

Founder-led B2B SaaS and AI companies whose product has outgrown its story.

Service 2

Go-to-market strategy

Choose the markets and segments worth winning, plan the launch and get sales and marketing telling one story.

Market selection→Audience priorities→Launch planning→Sales alignment

  • Market and segment scoring: size, access, fit and competition
  • Buyer and competitor research in the target market, including India ↔ US, UK and GCC moves
  • A 90-day launch plan with channels, owners and milestones
  • Sales narrative, objection handling and enablement aligned to the positioning

Go-to-market in detail

Typical engagement

Four to six weeks for a full plan; two weeks for a launch or market-entry sprint.

Best for

Companies launching a new product, entering a new region, or rebuilding how sales and marketing work together.

Service 3

Demand generation and growth

Build demand programmes that match how your buyers research and buy, and measure what actually moves pipeline.

Demand strategy→Campaigns→Conversion→Measurement

  • A demand strategy built on your buyer's research journey, not channel fashion
  • Campaign design: content, outbound and founder-led programmes that tell one story
  • Landing-page and funnel experiments with clear hypotheses
  • A measurement model that connects activity to pipeline, reviewed monthly

Demand generation in detail

Typical engagement

A three-week strategy phase, then an optional monthly programme with a three-month minimum.

Best for

Teams with a story that works and a pipeline that doesn't.

Service 4

AI search and content visibility

Understand how AI assistants and search engines describe your category, and earn a place in the answers buyers see.

Search research→Expert content→Discoverability→Visibility measurement

  • A visibility snapshot: how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google answer your buyers' prompts today
  • The sources those answers draw on, and the gaps you can fill
  • An expert-content plan that answers real buyer questions with real depth
  • A repeatable measurement routine, with its limits stated plainly

AI search visibility in detail

Typical engagement

A two-week snapshot and plan, then quarterly re-measurement.

Best for

Companies whose buyers start research in an AI assistant and whose category is still being defined there.
