Why Traxn exists.

Traxn was started on a simple observation: most marketing budgets are spent answering the wrong question. This page explains the thinking behind the consultancy, who's behind it, and how we work.

Our story

Too many briefs started with "we need more leads".

Traxn grew out of years of product marketing and go-to-market work inside B2B SaaS companies in India, selling at home and into the US, UK and Gulf. The same pattern kept repeating. A team would arrive with a channel problem, a content problem or a lead problem. Underneath, almost always, was a clarity problem: nobody had decided, with evidence, who the customer was and why they should choose this product over the alternative they already used.

Agencies rarely fixed it, because they're paid to execute. Freelancers rarely fixed it, because they're hired for a task. Full-time hires took months and often inherited the confusion.

Traxn is built to do the piece in between: the strategic work that makes everything after it cheaper, and the first layer of execution that proves it. Founder-led, so the person who does the thinking is the person on the call.

Our belief

Better marketing starts with better questions.

"How do we get more leads?" is a request. "Who is choosing us today, and why?" is a question. Only one of them leads somewhere.

Our job on every engagement is to ask the questions the team has been too busy, or too close, to ask. What does the buyer use instead of us? Why did the last ten deals close, really? What would have to be true for this market to work? The answers are almost always already in the company, in call recordings, lost-deal notes and support tickets. We go and find them.

Our philosophy

Two rules we don't break.

Clarity over complexity

A strategy that fits on one page gets used; a 60-slide deck gets filed. We give you three decisions rather than thirty recommendations, and we write everything so a new hire could pick it up and act on it.

Evidence over assumptions

We bring the customer's words before we bring our opinion. Every recommendation points to the call, review, data or lost deal it came from, so you can disagree with the evidence rather than with us.

Our perspective

What we think changes next.

AI collapses the cost of average

Content, ads and generic strategy decks are close to free now. That doesn't make marketing less valuable; it makes the parts AI can't do more valuable: talking to customers, making a real choice about focus, and having an opinion worth citing.

Customer discovery becomes the moat

When every competitor can produce the same output, the company that knows its buyers best wins. Discovery stops being a product-team ritual and becomes the marketing team's core skill.

Differentiation has to be true

Buyers now check claims in seconds, often by asking an AI. Positioning built on adjectives collapses. Positioning built on what you actually do differently holds up.

Discovery moves into the answer box

A growing share of B2B research starts as a question to an assistant and ends with a shortlist of three or four names. Being one of those names, and being described correctly, becomes a marketing objective in its own right.

Working principles

How we work with clients.

Fixed scope, fixed price

Every engagement has a written scope and a price agreed before work starts. No hourly surprises.

Your time, budgeted

We tell you upfront how many hours we need from your team and who should be in the room, then stick to it.

Evidence first, opinion second

We show you what buyers said before we tell you what we think. You can argue with the data.

We'll say no

If you don't need us, or need something we don't do, we'll say so on the first call and point you to someone who does.

Written to be used

Deliverables are short, specific and written so a new hire could act on them without us in the room.

Nothing published without permission

Client names, results and quotes appear on this site only when the client has approved them in writing.
