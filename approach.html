From first conversation to decisions you can act on.

Every Traxn engagement, whatever its size, runs through the same four stages. This page explains what happens in each, the research we rely on, how deliverables are agreed and what you should expect from working with us.

01Understand

Research the business, its customers and its market.

We start by learning what you already know and what your buyers already said. The goal is to replace assumptions with the customer's own words before anyone proposes anything.

  • Recorded sales and customer calls, lost-deal notes and support tickets
  • Reviews on G2, Capterra and app marketplaces, for you and for competitors
  • Five to eight customer or prospect interviews when existing evidence is thin
  • Competitor websites, pricing pages and sales decks
  • How Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini answer your buyers' questions
  • Your own data: closed-won sources, cycle length, win rate by segment

Output: a short evidence summary with quotes, not paraphrase.

02Define

Identify the problem and prioritise the opportunities.

Most teams arrive with a symptom. Here we name the cause, and rank what could be done about it by how much it's worth and how hard it is. This is where we'll argue for focus.

  • One problem statement the whole team agrees with
  • Two or three options, each with its upside, risk and evidence
  • A working session (usually 75 minutes) where your team chooses

Output: a decision, written down, with the reasons.

03Build

Create the strategy and an actionable roadmap.

We write the strategy short enough to be used, then the roadmap that turns it into work: owners, dates and the first things to ship. Where it proves the strategy, we write the first execution too, such as homepage copy, campaign briefs or outbound sequences.

  • One-page strategy document
  • 90-day roadmap with owners and milestones
  • First-layer execution agreed in the scope
  • One live review and one revision round

Output: a plan your team can start on Monday.

04Learn

Measure progress and refine the decisions.

Strategy is a hypothesis until the market responds. We define in advance the two or three signals that would show it's working, check them at 30, 60 and 90 days, and change course on evidence.

  • Leading indicators agreed before launch (reply rate, qualified first calls, time to first meeting)
  • Lagging indicators tied to your sales cycle (pipeline created, win rate, cycle length)
  • A review format that ends in one decision: scale, fix or stop

Output: a monthly or quarterly review that ends in one clear next step.

How we agree the work

Scope, price and deliverables, in writing.

One-page proposal

After the first call you get a single page: the problem as we understand it, the scope, the deliverables, your time commitment and a fixed price. Nothing starts until you've agreed it.

Deliverables, not hours

You're paying for named outputs, listed on each service page. If the scope needs to change midway, we re-agree it in writing before continuing.

Pricing and payment

Fixed price per engagement. Indian companies are invoiced in INR plus GST; companies elsewhere in USD. Payment schedule is set out in the proposal. Monthly programmes have a three-month minimum.

How we measure

Signals agreed before we start, read honestly afterwards.

We agree the measures of success in the proposal, before any work. Leading indicators tell you within weeks whether the direction is right; lagging indicators follow your sales cycle and tell you whether it paid off.

We measure the same way before and after, state the limits of each measure, and don't claim credit for movement we can't attribute. If something didn't work, the review says so and says what we'd change.

What we won't do is promise a number before we've seen your data, or report vanity metrics as results.
