AI search and content visibility

Be in the answer when buyers ask an AI.

More B2B research now starts as a question to ChatGPT, Perplexity or Gemini, and the answer names three or four vendors. We find out how those tools describe your category today, which sources they lean on, and what it takes to be one of the names.

Search research→Expert content→Discoverability→Visibility measurement

1 · The problem

The shortlist is being written before you know a deal exists.

A buyer types "best contract management tools for mid-size Indian companies" into an AI assistant. It answers with four names, a sentence on each, and a reason to pick one. If you're not in that answer, you're not in the deal. If you're in it but described wrongly, you're in the wrong deal.

Most companies have no idea what these tools say about them. Fewer still know which pages, reviews and articles the answers draw on, so they can't change them.

Example buyer prompt "What are the best tools for [your category] for a 200-person company in the UK?" What we check in the answer Are you named? In what position? How are you described, and does it match your positioning? Which sources does the answer cite or paraphrase? Which competitors appear instead of you, and why?

3 · Our process

Two weeks to a plan. Quarterly re-measurement.

Understand · Days 1–5

Research the answers

We build a prompt set from how your buyers actually ask, run it across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google, and record who's named, how, and from which sources.

Define · Days 6–7

Find the gaps

Where you're absent, where you're described wrongly, and which sources explain the difference between you and the vendors that are named.

Build · Days 8–10

Plan the content

An expert-content plan: the questions to answer, the depth and evidence each needs, where it should live, and the third-party sources to update or earn.

Learn · Quarterly

Re-measure

Same prompts, same method, each quarter. We report movement, not a score, because results vary by prompt, context and time.

For categories still being defined in the answers.

7 · Questions

Asked before most AI search projects.

Is this just SEO with a new name?

They overlap, because AI assistants draw on much of what search indexes. But the unit of success is different: being cited and described correctly in an answer, not ranking on a results page. Depth, evidence and third-party corroboration matter more; keyword volume matters less.

Can you guarantee we'll appear in ChatGPT's answers?

No, and we'd be wary of anyone who does. We can show you where you stand, what the named vendors have that you don't, and a plan to close the gap. Then we measure honestly.

Do you write the content?

We write the plan, the briefs and the first one or two pieces to set the standard. Your subject-matter experts are the source; we help them say it with depth. Larger volumes come from your team or writers we help you brief.

What does it cost?

The two-week snapshot and plan is fixed-price. Quarterly re-measurement is a small fixed fee. You get a one-page proposal after the first call.
