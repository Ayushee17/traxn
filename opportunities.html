Fit

Who this is for, and who it isn't.

We would rather lose a bad-fit project on the first call than three weeks into it. So, plainly:

A good fit

  • B2B SaaS and AI companies from seed to Series B
  • Founder-led teams whose product has outgrown its story
  • Companies moving between India and the US, UK or GCC
  • Marketing leaders who want a strategic partner, not extra hands
  • Teams willing to have their assumptions contradicted by evidence

Not a fit

  • Full execution agency work: ads, design, video production
  • Brand identity built from scratch
  • Anyone wanting a strategy that agrees with a decision already made
  • Projects where nobody can give us access to actual customers
  • Work that needs to start before a scope is agreed in writing

We'll say no.If you don't need us, or need something we don't do, we'll say so on the first call and point you to someone who does it well.
